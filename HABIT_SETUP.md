# HABIT.coo Shopify demo

This is a branded version of the Sora practice theme, not a second independent client project. Prepared with AI assistance.

## Installation
Upload `habit-theme.zip` to Shopify as a draft theme. ZIP directories are already at the archive root: do not extract before uploading.

Brand name and tagline can be edited under Theme settings → Brand. Header and footer share these settings. Hero supports an optional merchant-owned photograph; the fallback is an original illustrated gift box, not a photograph of a HABIT.coo product.

Select a collection under Featured. Shopify generated test products may be used for functionality checks; replace them with products and images you own before a final portfolio review. No HABIT.coo product specifications or prices have been invented.

## Current verification
Sora base was uploaded by the owner and rendered in the Shopify theme editor on 18 November 2026. This HABIT.coo revision has not yet been uploaded or tested in Shopify. Local static checks do not establish real cart or checkout behavior.

Before sharing, test homepage, product variants and sold-out state, quantity, gift message, cart update/removal, mobile layout, and reviewer access. Keep the store in development mode with test payments.
